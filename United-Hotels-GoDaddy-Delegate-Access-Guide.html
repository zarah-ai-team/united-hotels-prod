1.  Overview

This guide explains how to give our team the access needed to switch on email for the United Hotels platform. The domain is registered with GoDaddy under your account, so enabling platform email (transactional messages such as booking confirmations, password resets, and notifications) requires updating the domain’s DNS records.

GoDaddy’s Delegate Access feature lets you grant that permission securely — without sharing your password, payment details, or account PIN — and you can change or remove it at any time.

There are three parts:

  • Part A — You grant our team delegate access to the domain.
  • Part B — You confirm domain (folder) permissions so we can edit DNS records.
  • Part C — We add a small set of DNS records that authenticate and enable platform email.

The whole process on your side takes about 5–10 minutes.

2.  Before you begin

Please have the following ready:

  • Your GoDaddy sign-in — the username and password for the account that owns the domain. If two-step verification is enabled, keep your phone or authenticator app nearby.
  • Our delegate details — the exact name and email address of the GoDaddy account we want you to invite (provided in the table below).
  • Account owner access — only the account owner (or an existing full-access delegate) can invite new delegates.
Good to know

The person you invite must have a GoDaddy account to accept the invitation. If they don’t, GoDaddy will prompt them to create one for free.

A pending invitation expires if it isn’t accepted within roughly 48–72 hours, so it’s best to send it when our team is ready to act.

4.  Part A — Grant delegate access

Follow these steps in your GoDaddy account:

  1. Sign in to your GoDaddy account at account.godaddy.com.
  2. Open the Delegate Access page: go directly to account.godaddy.com/access, or select your profile / name (top-right) → Account Settings → Delegate Access.
  3. In the “People who can access my account” section, select Invite to Access.
  4. Enter the Name and Email address exactly as we provided. The email must match our team member’s GoDaddy account.
  5. Choose the access level — select Domains Only (recommended) or Products & Domains. See Section 5 for guidance.
  6. Select Invite. GoDaddy emails an invitation to our team, who will accept it to activate access.

5.  Choosing the right access level

When you invite a delegate, GoDaddy asks you to pick one access level. For enabling email, DNS access is all we need.

Access levelWhat the delegate can doUse for United Hotels?
Domains OnlyManage and/or transfer domains only — DNS records, nameservers, forwarding. No access to other products.Recommended — least privilege; sufficient to enable email.
Products & DomainsEverything above, plus access to product control panels (e.g., hosting). Cannot make purchases.Optional — only if we will also manage hosting or other GoDaddy products for you.
Products, Domains, & PurchaseEverything above, plus the ability to buy/renew using your stored payment method.Not needed — grants more than DNS work requires.
Accounts Connection OnlyStays connected for future work, but cannot currently access or purchase products.Not needed for this task.

Whichever level you choose, a delegate can never do the following — so your account stays protected:

  • View or change your password or Support PIN.
  • View or add payment methods, or see your order history.
  • Invite other people to access your account.
  • Accept an incoming domain transfer, or upgrade products.

6.  Part B — Confirm domain permissions (so DNS can be edited)

Delegates are granted domain Management Access by default, which is what allows DNS records to be edited. Use these steps to review or restrict which domains we can manage — recommended if your account holds more than one domain.

  1. Sign in to your GoDaddy Domain Portfolio.
  2. Select the Folders icon above your domain list, choose the folder that contains yourdomain.com (or All Domains), then select Edit Folder.
  3. Set Management Access to ON (required to edit DNS). Leave Transfer Access OFF — we don’t need to transfer, delete, or sell domains.
  4. Select Save.
PermissionWhat it allows (and whether we need it)
Management Access (needed)Edit DNS records, nameservers, hostnames and forwarding; manage subdomains; lock/unlock; auto-renew on/off; update contact info.
Transfer Access (leave off)Initiate domain transfers to another registrar or account, delete domains, or list a domain for sale. Not required for email.

7.  What happens after you send the invite

  • Our team accepts the emailed invitation. Your account then appears for us under “Accounts I can access.”
  • We add the DNS records listed in Part C using the exact values issued by the email provider for United Hotels’ domain.
  • Verification & propagation — DNS changes usually take effect within minutes but can take up to 48 hours to fully propagate. We verify the domain with the provider before going live.
  • We confirm back to you once platform email is authenticated and fully enabled.

8.  Part C — DNS records we add to enable email

United Hotels’ platform sends email through a transactional email service (for example AWS SES, SendGrid, Mailgun, or Postmark). To authenticate that mail and keep it out of spam, we add the record types below. The exact hosts and values are generated in the provider’s dashboard — the examples here are illustrative.

TypeHost / Name (example)Example valuePurpose
TXT_amazonses(provider-issued token)Domain verification — proves to the provider that we control the domain.
CNAME ×3xxxx._domainkeyxxxx.dkim.amazonses.comDKIM — digitally signs outgoing mail so it can’t be spoofed.
TXT@ (root)v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~allSPF — authorises the provider to send mail for your domain.
MXsend (subdomain)feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com (priority 10)Custom “MAIL FROM” — routes bounces and complaints correctly.
TXT_dmarcv=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.comDMARC — sets policy for unauthenticated mail and enables reports.
CNAME (optional)email(provider tracking host)Link/open tracking for outbound email (if used).
Entering records in GoDaddy — two tips

The Host / Name field is relative to your domain. For a record on the root, enter @; for send.yourdomain.com enter only send — GoDaddy adds the domain automatically.

Copy each value exactly from the provider — no extra spaces or trailing dots. SendGrid, Mailgun and Postmark use their own hosts (e.g. em1234, k1._domainkey), which we’ll supply.

9.  Security & privacy

  • No password sharing. Delegate access never exposes your password, Support PIN, or payment methods.
  • Least privilege. We request only Domains Only access with Management Access — enough to manage DNS and nothing more.
  • You stay the owner. You retain full control and can review or revoke access at any time.
  • Extra protection. We recommend enabling two-step verification on your GoDaddy account if it isn’t already on.

10.  Managing or revoking access later

You can change our access level or remove it entirely at any time: go to account.godaddy.com/access, find our team under “People who can access my account,” and select the option to change the access level or delete the delegate. Removing access does not affect DNS records already in place.
